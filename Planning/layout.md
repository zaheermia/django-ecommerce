# Layout

Every page uses `base.html` with a nav bar at the top.

## Nav bar
- Everyone: Home | Vendors | Stores | Products | Reddit
- Vendor also gets: My Stores | My Products | Reviews
- Buyer also gets: Cart | My Orders | My Reviews
- Then Login and Register, or Logout

## Pages
- Home - links to all the main pages
- Vendors - all vendors, click one to see their stores
- Stores - all stores, click one to see its products
- Products - all products, click one to see its reviews
- My Stores - the vendor's stores with Add Product, Edit and Delete
- My Products - the vendor's products with Add Product, Edit and Delete
- Reviews (vendor) - all reviews for their products with a summary
- My Reviews (buyer) - their reviews with Edit and Delete and a summary
- Cart, My Orders and the invoice page
- Register, login and password pages
- Reddit - posts from r/django
